# SamudraSense: Climate-Centred Demonstration Storyboard

## 1. Problem Statement

The Gulf of Mannar Biosphere Reserve and Marine National Park comprises 21 islands along the southeastern coast of Tamil Nadu, India, harbouring globally significant marine biodiversity including coral reef ecosystems, extensive seagrass meadows, and vulnerable species such as the dugong (*Dugong dugon*). These critical coastal habitats serve as vital marine carbon sinks and primary fish breeding nurseries.

Coastal monitoring and fisheries surveillance authorities operate under severe constraints: vast maritime surface areas, intermittent communication infrastructure, and disconnected data feeds. Analysts must manually cross-reference AIS vessel streams, environmental readings, and satellite observations across disparate sources. In unmonitored waters, illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing and unauthorised loitering in designated marine park buffers damage benthic habitats, deplete juvenile fish populations, and erode ecosystem stability.

Today's existing commercial surveillance tools are often cloud-dependent, cost-prohibitive, and present opaque risk scores without verifiable evidence chains. Furthermore, raw tracking gaps are frequently misconstrued without receiver health context or local marine weather conditions.

SamudraSense addresses this operational challenge through a lightweight, self-contained coastal monitoring workstation. It joins regional ocean observations, explainable rule-based alert triage, and immutable evidence preparation in a single unified interface:

- **Truthful Data Provenance:** Every displayed record explicitly states whether its source is live, historical, cached, or synthetic. Missing data is never hidden or assumed clean.
- **Rule-Based, Explainable Risk Engine:** Vessel risk scores are computed using transparent, additive scoring rules (AIS gaps, protected zone proximity, loitering, and encounters). Scores are explainable, and clear counterfactual conditions ("what would clear it") are provided.
- **Operational Context Without False Causality:** Regional environmental values (sea-surface temperature and chlorophyll) and localized Open-Meteo marine weather provide operational context for triage. They never modify risk scores and never imply direct causality for vessel maneuvers.
- **Verifiable Evidence and Case Integrity:** Each investigation captures an immutable evidence snapshot anchored by a local SHA-256 hash. Review and sign-off workflows ensure procedural transparency before export.

### Expected Ecological Pathway

Better surveillance capability does not produce an immediate, directly observed reduction in carbon emissions or an immediate decline in regional fishing pressure. Rather, SamudraSense establishes a realistic operational pathway:
1. Enabling analysts to review and substantiate suspicious incursions in protected zones in `[measure at B-13]` minutes (compared to raw multi-file inspection).
2. Distinguishing plausible environmental sheltering from intentional silent incursions using weather and receiver coverage checks.
3. Providing tamper-evident case packages suitable for administrative action.

Consistent, evidence-backed enforcement deters destructive incursions into critical seagrass and reef nurseries, thereby fostering long-term marine ecosystem resilience and conserving natural blue-carbon sequestration assets.
