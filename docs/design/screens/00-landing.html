How it works

Predict. Detect. Act.

01

Predict

  • Sea-temperature forecasts per grid cell
  • Rank where illegal fishing moves next
  • Scan only high-risk satellite tiles
02

Detect

  • Radar hulls with no AIS become dark vessels
  • Weather explains slow ships before alarms
  • Map who a dark ship met in 48 hours
03

Act

  • Evidence is hashed and kept
  • A local LLM drafts the report
  • Nothing is sent without an officer
Explainable by design

Every alert says why.

An officer can't board a boat because a model said 0.87. SamudraSense shows exactly what added up to the score, what it ruled out, and where every number came from.

The score is the explanation

Each behaviour adds points. No hidden weights.

Ruled out, not ignored

Storms and receiver outages are checked and shown.
