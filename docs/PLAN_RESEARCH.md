# SamudraSense: source and competitive evidence for the delivery plan

Checked 2 November 2026 using Firecrawl and primary provider pages. This document supports the delivery plan; it does not claim that any provider account, regional coverage, production quota or commercial permission has been obtained. Prices and terms are snapshots. Open-source software, freely accessible data and free hosted API access are different things.

## What the competitive gap actually is

| Existing product | Verified capability | Consequence for our plan |
|---|---|---|
| [Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/documentation/) | Vessel identity, fishing activity, encounters, vessel detections and risk information through APIs. Its [version 5 release](https://globalfishingwatch.org/platform-update/version-5-data-pipeline-release/) was published on 21 October 2026. | Combining AIS and satellite detections is established. Pin dataset versions and keep source metadata; avoid claiming novelty for combining feeds. |
| [Skylight / Ai2](https://allenai.org/skylight), [program access](https://skylight.global/) | Satellite imagery and vessel behavior analysis. Access is free to eligible government, regional and nongovernmental organizations. [Detection filters](https://support.skylight.global/en_US/vessel-detection-filters) already distinguish AIS-correlated and uncorrelated detections. | Low price alone is not enough differentiation. An uncorrelated detection has several possible explanations; it is not proof of deliberate evasion. |
| [OceanMind Precision Regulation](https://oceanmind.global/products-services/precision-regulation/) | Satellite and machine-learning vessel behavior analysis, expert support, operational risk assessment, and risk models tailored to national regulations. | Local rules and analyst review also exist elsewhere. Focus on making a small regional workflow demonstrably useful, transparent and easy to operate. |
| [Windward](https://windward.ai/) | Markets multi-sensor intelligence and behavioral analytics with explainable, shareable outputs. | Do not claim that explainability itself is unique. A student team cannot match its global collection capability within this release. |

Proposed differentiation is a hypothesis to test: a self-hostable regional investigation workspace that combines transparent risk rules, explicit missing-data/coverage explanations, weather context, local restrictions, and reproducible evidence exports, while remaining usable on modest hardware and weak connections. Measure analyst time, correctness of explanations, false alerts and operating cost. No reviewed source proves this combination is unique or that SamudraSense already outperforms these products.

### Important correction for the SAR evaluation

The Ai2 repository says its initial training uses the 50 xView3 validation scenes and later stages incorporate xView3 training scenes. Therefore downloading an arbitrary scene called "validation" does **not** establish that it was held out from the published model. Check its training split. Use genuinely unseen labeled scenes for accuracy claims. If overlap cannot be ruled out, call the result an integration smoke test and report runtime, not independent generalization accuracy. Keep scene-separated splits for all future training.

### Access gates that should happen early

1. Test one AISStream regional session and store counts, freshness, disconnects and observed spatial coverage. An empty feed is a coverage result, not proof that no vessels are present.
2. Test one authenticated GFW request, inspect its returned geometry and timestamps, and record permissions. Confirm whether point detections are available to this account before promising point-level fusion.
3. Choose one licensed regional boundary source. If unavailable, keep clearly marked reference/demo areas and generic user-defined monitoring zones; never present an invented legal restriction.
4. Choose exact Copernicus Marine products and download a small regional subset. Check issue/valid times and units before using any variable in a score or trajectory.
5. Freeze a tiny versioned replay set that can be redistributed. Keep it separate from live records and restricted source data.

## Scope recommendations for a six-week release

Finish the investigation workflow first: authenticated users, live/replay source status, regional map, explainable alerts, uncertainty-aware SAR candidates, review/assignment, event timeline, evidence snapshots and export, audit trail, backup/restore and fault handling. Keep PostGIS and existing task infrastructure. A second database or a new queue will consume delivery time without closing the identified product gaps.

Ocean conditions can support a basic transparent hotspot experiment, provided it is labeled by its target: predicted fishing activity or patrol priority, not fish abundance, climate prediction, or illegality. Compare a time-separated forecast against a simple recent-activity baseline; if it does not improve, ship the baseline with an honest label. Weather context should not automatically clear a suspicious event without validated rules.

Make spill work a bounded scenario module if it is part of the required final demo. Defer automatic oil detection, arbitrary ocean-wide spill forecasting and emergency-use claims. Defer full SAR retraining, global live AIS guarantees, commercial satellite tasking, native mobile apps, port-wide ownership intelligence and autonomous enforcement. These are separate capabilities with large data, access or evaluation dependencies.

Useful acceptance measurements: source freshness visible on every view; no fake point detections from grid data; no risk change when weather is unavailable; replay produces repeatable event evidence; unauthorized users cannot alter a report; export preserves input IDs, times and algorithm versions; source outages preserve saved cases; the declared dataset fits the agreed disk budget.

## Research limitations

No credentials were used to exercise provider data APIs. Regional AIS completeness, GFW point-download permission and end-to-end latency remain open integration checks. No commercial permissions were obtained. Product claims above are provider descriptions, not independent performance evaluations. Local authoritative restriction datasets and language review require project-owner/domain input. The six-week feasibility recommendation is an engineering estimate, not a delivery guarantee.

Research artifacts are under ignored `.firecrawl/plan-*` files. Existing source extracts from 1 November were reused where appropriate; current key API/licence and competitor pages were checked again on 2 November. No dataset or model weights were downloaded.
